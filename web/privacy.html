Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Last Updated: November 10, 2025

Welcome to FODMAP LOGMAP ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled by the app.

1. What information do we collect?

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide and store it directly on your device. This includes:

  • Profile Information: Any health conditions, intolerances, allergies, and dietary preferences you provide (e.g., "IMO", "Sorbitol intolerance", "Vegetarian").
  • Log Entries: All data you log, including dates, food names, doses, FODMAP groups, symptoms, severity ratings, and personal notes.
  • Phase & Medication Settings: Your current phase, start dates, durations, and any medication schedules you create, including medication names, times, and adherence logs.
  • AI API Key: Your personal Google AI API key.

2. How is your information stored?

All information listed above is stored only on your device in your browser's local storage (localStorage).

  • We do not have a server.
  • We do not see, collect, or transmit your personal data.
  • Your data does not leave your device, with one exception (see Section 3).

3. When is your information shared?

Your data is only shared in one specific circumstance, which you must initiate:

  • AI Assistant (Google AI): When you use any AI-powered features, such as the "AI Assistant", "Plan My Challenge" and "Summarize My Journey", the app sends specific data to Google to generate a response. This may include:
    • Your profile information (intolerances, diagnoses) to personalize the response.
    • Your log entries (for the "Summarize" feature).
    • The food name or image you submitted.
  • This data is sent directly from your device to Google's API. We do not receive it. Your use of this feature is subject to Google's API Privacy Policy.

4. How can you control your data?

You have full control over your data.

  • View & Edit: You can view and edit all your log entries and profile data at any time within the app.
  • Delete: You can delete individual log entries from the "Daily Log" tab.
  • Full Deletion: You can delete all of your data (logs and profile) by using the "Reset App" button in the Profile tab. This action clears the app's localStorage on your device and cannot be undone.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
