Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Last Updated: November 19, 2025

Welcome to FODMAP LOGMAP. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data, whether you use the app as a Guest or with a Registered Account.

1. Types of Users & Data Storage

A. Guest Users (Unregistered)
If you use the app without creating an account, all your data (logs, profile, settings) is stored exclusively on your device (localStorage). We do not have access to it.

B. Registered Users (Account Holders)
If you choose to create an account to sync your data across devices, your data is stored securely in the cloud using Google Firebase. This includes:

  • Account Data: Email address, display name, and authentication credentials.
  • Health Data: Logs, symptoms, diagnoses, and dietary preferences.

2. What Information Do We Collect?

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: Health conditions, food logs, symptom severity, and medication schedules.

3. Third-Party Service Providers (Data Processors)

We use trusted third-party services to operate the app. Your data is shared with them only as necessary:

  • Google Firebase (USA): Provides authentication and database hosting. Firebase encrypts data in transit and at rest.
  • Google Gemini AI: When you use the "AI Assistant," specific queries (anonymized where possible) are sent to Google's AI API to generate responses.

4. Your Rights (GDPR Compliance)

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can view your data in the app at any time. You can export a full JSON copy of your data via "Profile > Data Management > Export Data".
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit or delete individual log entries and update your profile settings instantly within the app.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):
    • Guests: Use the "Reset App" button to wipe all local data.
    • Accounts: Use the "Delete Account" button in "Account Settings". This permanently deletes your authentication record and all database entries.
  • Legal Basis: We process your health data based on your explicit consent when you voluntarily log it. We process your account data to fulfill the contract of providing cross-device syncing.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as you use the app. If you delete your account, your data is immediately removed from our live database.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data rights, please contact us.
