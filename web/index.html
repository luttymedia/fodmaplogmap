Your Toolkit for a Happy Gut

We built FodLog with features that actually help you find your triggers—without the overwhelm.

The AI Food Assistant

Instantly check any food. Take a photo of your meal or an ingredient list. Our AI Assistant gives you personalized advice based on your unique profile.

Guided Reintroduction Steps

We guide you through reintroducing each FODMAP group (Fructose, Sorbitol, etc.) one by one. Clear instructions, simple challenges, and zero guesswork.

Your Personal 'Forever' List

As you complete challenges, the app builds your personal "Safe" and "Limit" list. It's your unique food map, built by you, for you. This is the ultimate goal!

Get started in 3 easy steps

1. Log Your Day

Quickly log meals, tap any symptoms, and set a fast 1-5 severity.

2. Start a Challenge

Pick a FODMAP group and follow the simple, guided reintroduction steps.

3. Find Your Foods

Our app auto-scans your log to help build your personal, permanent "Safe List".
