🚀 FODMAP Journey!

🔬 What are FODMAPs?

Fermentable Oligosaccharides, Disaccharides, Monosaccharides, And Polyols.

Short-chain carbs poorly absorbed in the small intestine. They draw water and ferment in the large intestine, causing gas, bloating, etc.

🗺️ The 4 Phases

Phase 1: Pre-Treatment

🛡️ Follow specific diet rules to calm symptoms before treatment.

Phase 2: Restriction

🚫 Remove high-FODMAP foods to calm symptoms.

Phase 3: Reintroduction

🧪 Systematically reintroduce groups to identify triggers (use the Reintro Log!).

Phase 4: Personalization

🧘 Build a long-term diet including tolerated foods.

Your All-in-One Companion

This app is designed to help you at every stage of your journey with three key tools:

  • 1. A Smart, Adaptive Log

    Track meals, symptoms, and reintroduction challenges. The app's Home screen and log form adapt to your current phase.

  • 2. A Powerful AI Assistant

    Instantly check if a food is safe, analyze ingredient lists from a photo, and get personalized advice based on your profile.

  • 3. A Personalization Hub

    As you log your triggers and safe foods, the app "auto-scans" your history to build your final, long-term personalized diet.
